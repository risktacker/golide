Frequently asked questions

Does this guarantee interviews or a job?

No. It improves decisions, evidence quality and consistency. Hiring outcomes remain outside your control.

Is this a way to beat an ATS?

No. The method helps a human reader see truthful relevance. It does not promise to manipulate screening systems.

What score should I apply with?

Use 6/12 as the minimum working threshold. Skip any role with a hard legal, location, credential or practical barrier.

Can I use AI?

Yes, to organise and improve facts you provide. Never let it invent titles, tools, metrics, qualifications or results.

Where is my information stored?

This edition stores your answers only in this browser on this device. Export the tracker if you need a backup.

How long should the process take?

Your first score should take about 20 minutes. A defensible tailored application may take longer; speed improves as your evidence bank grows.

What if I have little work experience?

Use honest evidence from projects, study, volunteering, internships and responsibilities. Do not turn related exposure into professional experience.

How do I get support?

Use the Support area inside Easy With Golide on Whop and include the step where you are stuck.
